# H2 (FR): buying the end of forced deleveraging in BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP on Coinbase perps

**Verdict: FAILS.** The frozen variant fails G1–G4 at 2× costs and passes only G5. All 13 DEV trials had a negative DEV
alpha t. The gross rebound after a cascade is real but small: 5–27 bp per trade (DEV, best variants). That is less than
2× perp taker costs (about 22–40 bp round trip), and less than 1× costs on the non-AI variants.

## Mechanism
A long-liquidation cascade forces sales until the forced flow is exhausted. Then the price overshoots and partly snaps
back. We take the other side with a perp taker order at the first sign of exhaustion. The counterparty is leveraged
longs who are being liquidated.

## Deviations
1. **R3 venue facts applied before any result** (coordinator):
   - Friday 5–6 pm ET halt.
   - 2-minute halts on ±10% moves from the hourly reference, emulated before launch.
   - Fair Value Limit ±5% on the take-profit: it delayed 1 trade.
   - Fee sensitivity at $0.12 and $0.30.
   - 4 pm ET table.
2. **Perp launch** is the first full UTC day of prints.
3. **The take-profit is translated by the perp/spot basis** at the decision minute. It is never placed at or below
   entry.
4. **No HOLD statistic was computed before the freeze push.** After the HOLD read, only descriptive scripts were added:
   prints.py and diag.py.
5. **Interpretation:** a trade is skipped when the decision close is already at or above the take-profit.

## Weaknesses
- The cost-free gross edge is about 15–30 bp, under the 2× cost of 22–40 bp round trip.
- The AI filter turns a −4.1 t into −0.95 on DEV. That is selection noise around zero, not an edge.
- 2022 contracts made BTC 2× costs 40–50 bp.
- The perp era (Jul 2025 on) is short: 53 trades.

## Shared-code note
G4's v_z is the variance of z across all ledger trials. Large negative trials, such as h2fr's (z down to −8.6), inflate
it from the 1.126 floor to 7.7–9.3. That makes SR0 about 5.5 for every idea, and G4 then fails for everyone. This was
reported to the coordinator. It does not change h2fr's verdict.
