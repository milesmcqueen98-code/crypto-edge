# H6 (VD): Coinbase dislocation bids against Bitstamp / perp fair value: REPORT

**Verdict: FAILS.** All 15 DEV trials lose money, even before fees. The frozen variant fails every gate (G1 to G5)
in DEV, in HOLD and over all days, at 2x costs and also at 1x. The bids are adversely selected: at a 1-minute
horizon Coinbase leads Bitstamp and the Coinbase perps, so most "gaps" are a stale reference, not a Coinbase-only
seller who gets reversed.

## Mechanism (SPEC, unchanged)
- Bid on Coinbase spot at ref_close(t-1) * exp(gbar - k * sigma_e).
  - gbar: the trailing 24h median of the log gap g = log(CB close / ref close).
  - sigma_e: the trailing 3-day std of g - gbar. Both use information through t-1.
  - Freshness guard: the reference must have traded in t-1.
- Fill: the minute opens above the bid and trades at least one tick through it.
  - Size: min($5K, theta * through-volume), theta = 0.5. Through-volume spreads each candle's volume over its range;
    the scan lasts up to 15 minutes while the price stays below the bid.
- Exits:
  - Post-only take-profit 60% of the way back to fair value.
  - Otherwise a market exit at the open of the first traded minute >= t+H+1.
- Costs (common.spot_cost_bp): maker 6 bp; taker 12.5 bp + 2 bp half-spread. 2x for the gates.
- Book: one position per coin, at most 6 open. Resting bids count at full size against cash.

## Data actually used
- **Coinbase spot:** 1-minute candles (data/minute) for 34 alts plus BTC (BTC only for the quiet filter), 2024-01-01
  to 2026-11-05. 2024 is warm-up only.

- **Scored days:**
  - Bitstamp variants: 2025-01-01 to 2026-05-31 in DEV (516 days).
  - Perp variants: 2025-07-19 to 2026-05-31 in DEV (317 days).
  - Frozen variant: 2025-07-19 to 2026-11-05 (475 days, of which 158 are HOLD).

How the grid ran:
- Core grid: k in {3, 4, 6} x reference in {Bitstamp, perp} x H in {30, 60}.
- Quiet filter on the best core variant: no bid while BTC's 15-minute move is beyond 2 sigma.
- Two AI variants on the better of those two:
  - LightGBM with the SPEC's features; walk-forward by quarter.
  - A model needs at least 6 months of candidate history, so for the perp reference the first model is 2026Q2.
  - Bids allowed when pred > 0 ("pos") or pred >= the training median ("q50").
- Net bp is very flat across k (-30 to -34 at 2x). A deeper bid only trades less.

## Years, capacity, P&L anatomy
**Fills by year:**
- 2025 (from Jul 19): 3,486 fills, $17.2M, -31.4 bp at 2x.
- 2026: 3,587 fills, $17.0M, -29.6 bp at 2x.
- The sleeve on $30K loses 179% (2025) and 168% (2026) of its capital (simple sums at 2x). A live sleeve would be
  wiped out within months.

**HOLD:** 1,086 fills, $4.8M, -28.3 bp at 2x and -13.8 bp at 1x.

**Capacity (theta 0.5):**
- Every order uses its full $5K: 7,073 fills, $34.2M, average $4,829, 93.7% at full size.
- At theta 0.25: 7,020 fills, 89.9% at full size.
- Only 201 fills went unplaced for lack of a slot.
- Capacity is not the constraint; every deployed dollar loses.

**Anatomy (all fills, bp of notional):**
- Fills are profitable often but lose big when wrong:
  - 75% of dollars exit at the take-profit, for +17.1 bp.
  - The time-exit remainder gives -19.3 bp.
- Gross is -2.2 bp, before 14.1 bp of fees at 1x (28.3 at 2x).
- Depth and take-profit: the median bid sits 32.6 bp under fair value, and the take-profit earns 19.8 bp. That is too
  thin to carry 12 bp of round-trip maker fees (24 bp at 2x).
- Time exits: mean -75 bp, median -50 bp; 5th-percentile adverse excursion -186 bp.
- By coin: ETH, SOL and XRP are 82% of the dollars. 18 of the 19 coins lose at 2x; the exception is AAVE (8 fills, +$7).

**Which venue moved first** (the 6 minutes before the fill: Coinbase falling to the bid vs the reference rising):
- Bitstamp reference: Coinbase-led 46% (k=3) to 65% (k=6).
- Perp reference: 52% to 79%.
- Frozen variant:
  - 78% Coinbase-led, at -30.6 bp.
  - 19% reference-led, at -28.8 bp.
  - 2% with no clear leader, at -41.0 bp.
- So it is not just thin Bitstamp moving: both kinds lose.

**Lead-lag of 1-minute returns** (DEV scored days):
- Coinbase leads every reference: corr(CB_t, ref_t+1) is 0.06 to 0.28.
- The reverse, corr(ref_t, CB_t+1), is -0.03 to +0.05 (ARB 0.16 is the exception).
- So Coinbase is the price-discovery venue and the reference is the stale one. A gap often closes by the reference
  moving: the reference does more than half the closing in 45% of frozen fills.

**Top-3 days' share of P&L:** not meaningful, because total P&L is negative.
- The three best days offset 0.35% of the loss.
- 305 losing days vs 46 winning days.

**Correlation with the incumbent** (daily, sleeve 2x vs incumbent at $100K): -0.36.
- The sleeve loses most on the volatile days the incumbent wins.
- On the incumbent's 20 worst days, the sleeve lost 37% of its capital.

**Other modelling notes:**
- **Latency:** the levels use t-1 candles (1-minute-old information).
- **Post-only rejection:** 0.07% of bids sat at or above the last Coinbase trade, and could be rejected as post-only.
- **Market exits:** they pay common.py's 2 bp half-spread. The pre-registration text says "1.5x the half-spread";
  the difference is under 0.3 bp per trade.

## Weaknesses and engineering choices
- **Choices fixed before any result:**
  - With $5K bids on $30K, at most 6 bids and positions can coexist. Bid slots go each minute to the free eligible
    coins with the most negative gap z at t-1.
  - Spot ticks are inferred per coin and month from Coinbase prices (APT and SEI changed in 2025Q4).
  - The 24h median needs 30 observations and the 3-day sigma needs 100.
  - The take-profit position is held until its last part sells.
  - Daily P&L books the take-profit part on its day and the remainder on the exit day.
- **AI:** the AI filter could only be trained from 2026Q2 for the perp reference (the 6-month history rule).
  - In DEV it predicted positive on 3.4% of the candidates it scored.
  - Rank IC 0.16, but the bids it allowed still averaged -22 bp at 2x.
- **Candles only for the levels:** a sub-minute bot would see the reference's own lag. That would not remove the
  adverse selection; it would sharpen it.
- **No redesign was done after HOLD.** There were no bug fixes after the freeze.

## Verdict
**FAIL.** The pre-registered mechanism does not exist at a tradable size at this latency:
- Gross P&L per fill is about zero to negative for every k, both references and both holds.
- Costs at 1x (14 bp) and 2x (28 bp) make it deeply negative.
- The idea is uncorrelated with R2-08 in timing (1% overlap), but that does not help a sleeve that loses about 30 bp
  on every dollar.
- Recommend: drop H6. The useful by-product is the lead-lag table: Coinbase leads Bitstamp and the Coinbase perps at
  1 minute, which argues against using them as live fair-value anchors for Coinbase spot bids.
