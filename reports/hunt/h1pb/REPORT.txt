# H1 (PB): perp-only wicks and rips on Coinbase US perps, fills checked on trade prints

**Verdict: FAIL at 2× costs (Path B).** G1 t = 2.07, G2 HOLD t = −0.16 / gain −0.17, G3 gain +0.43, G4 DSR 0.00
(provisional), G5 pass. The perp-only wicks are real and fill on prints. But after 2× fees they are a thin, fee- and
capacity-sensitive edge confined to 4:55–6:05 pm ET, not the Sharpe 5–9 the candle replays showed.

## Mechanism
Forced flow (FCM liquidations, stops, market orders) sweeps thin CDE perp books away from the spot-implied price. A
resting order kσ_e beyond fair value F (spot × trailing-24 h median basis) supplies liquidity and is paid when the
perp converges back to spot.

## Data actually used
- **Perp prints:** 33.5M CDE perp prints (20 perps), fetched only in candidate windows (k = 3 superset from 1-minute
  candles, both sides, both latency modes). Print contracts vs candle contracts: 0.997 on DEV, 1.000 on HOLD. No
  perp second was lost to the 1,000-print page cap.
- **Spot prints:** 298M Coinbase spot prints, for the spot-synchronous variant: candidate minutes t−1..t only. At most
  201 seconds lost to the 1,000-print page cap (BTC/ETH).
- **Fetch:** 478K requests over 44 Actions runs (DEV 16 shards plus 12 sub-shards; a v2 rerun of 10; HOLD 3).
- **First days:** BTC/ETH from Jul 19 2025; SOL/XRP Aug 16 2025; the Dec 12 alts from about Dec 15; the Feb 28 alts
  from about Mar 3; HYPE from June 2026 (HOLD only).
- **Scored days:** Jul 19 2025 – Nov 5 2026 (475: DEV 317, HOLD 158).
- **Fill rules** (prints):
  - A bid at L fills at L when a print is ≥ 1 tick through L and the previous print is at or above L (the post-only
    order could rest).
  - Size = min($10K / contract value, ⌊θ × contracts of the consecutive prints beyond L⌋), θ = 0.5.
  - The take-profit (60% back to F, post-only) is placed 1 s after the fill. It fills ⌊θ × through-volume⌋ on runs
    beyond it that start after it is live.
  - Time exit: the first print ≥ 2 s after fill + 60 min, plus the taker fee and 1.5× the half-spread.
- **Venue rules (R3):** orders > 5% from spot are skipped; halts and the Friday 5–6 pm ET halt have no prints, hence
  no fills.
- **Costs:** common.py, $0.20/contract/side. Slots: 1 per coin and side, 6 open, gross ≤ 3× capital.

## Fill-realism checks run
1. Print contracts vs candle volume, per chunk (above).
2. Kernel unit tests on hand-built tapes.
3. An independent plain-Python recomputation of 70 frozen trades (36 DEV, 34 HOLD, plus the 5 biggest wins and
   losses) from freshly re-fetched prints with the shared fetcher. **70/70 match exactly** (size, take-profit fills,
   gross, close time).

## Weaknesses
- **Fees decide the sign.** $0.12 → strong; $0.30 → negative. The all-in CFM fee is unverified (R3).
- **Capacity:** queue position and market-maker competition are proxied only by θ; θ = 0.25 fails.
- **Selection was not out-of-sample.** The 5–6 pm ET window was already flagged by the earlier perp replay. The
  frozen variant is the pre-registered maintenance split of the best core variant, and HOLD shows no incremental alpha.
- **Decay:** alt P&L fell after June 2026.
- **Short history:** 475 days.
- **No book history:** the rest condition is proxied by the previous print. Friday 5:59:30–6:00 no-cancel is not
  modelled beyond prints.

## Deviations (all logged)
1. **R3 venue facts** (coordinator): ±5% Fair Value Limit skip; no fills in halts; ET-hour table; fee sensitivity.
2. **Shared fetcher paging bug.** Every fill came from fetch.Fetcher, a common.TradeFetcher subclass with the same
   boundary-second fix the coordinator later shipped, checked against candle volume. No fill used the buggy fetcher.
3. **Level inputs v2, fixed after seeing DEV trigger and confirmation counts but before any DEV P&L.**
   - v1 forward-filled stale b̄/σ_e over windows that were too thin.
   - Its 720-minute floor blanked 12–52% of thin-alt time, contrary to "from its first day of trading".
   - v2 uses floors of 20 (24 h median) and 100 (3-day std) both-traded minutes, with no stale carry.
   - Alts were rerun in full; the majors were rerun on their 3.6–5.7K changed rows (first 3–5 days after listing),
     with 0 level flips elsewhere.
4. **HOLD fetch restricted to the frozen variant's own candidate superset** (exact for it). DEV/HOLD spliced at
   2026-05-31 10:00 UTC.
5. **Engineering choices:**
   - Rest condition via the previous print.
   - Minute-mode order live from t + 1 s to t + 61 s.
   - Take-profit rounded toward L.
   - The whole trade's P&L booked on the final close day.
   - Shards 14 and 15 split into 6 sub-runs each (same plan).

## Shared-code problems
1. common.TradeFetcher dropped prints at page boundaries (fixed by the coordinator in bf70666; I measured BTC perp
   prints 2–5% short in busy hours, spot about 10%).
2. ISO-8601 time parsing broke under pandas 3 (39aca18).
3. common.PERP gives SHIB a tick of 1e-9 per coin; the listing implies 1e-8 (no SHIB data, so no effect). PEPE is
   quoted per 1,000 in candles and prints (handled).
4. gates.py G4 uses a plain v_z inflated by extreme trials. My 18 losing grid trials add to that. G4 is recomputed
   centrally.

## Verdict
**FAIL.** Earlier candle replays (Sharpe 5–9) are not reproduced on prints. The fills are real: 99.8% of candle fills
are confirmed at the price, and 70/70 audited trades match. But the only profitable piece is perp-only dislocations
in the 5–6 pm ET window, mostly on alt perps. At the pre-registered 2× costs it adds +0.43 Sharpe to the book (HOLD
−0.17).

Re-test only as a new pre-registered idea, and only if an order preview shows the all-in fee is at most about
$0.12/contract. That test should exclude contracts whose fee exceeds about 5 bp (ETH).
