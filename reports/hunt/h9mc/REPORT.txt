# H9 (MC): the 4 pm ET margin step-up on Coinbase US perps

**Verdict: FAIL.** The forced flow is real on DEV, and Step 1 passes both tests. But it moves the basis only 2–3 bp, against round trips of 9–40 bp at 2× costs. All 19 trading trials had a negative DEV alpha t, and the frozen variant lost on HOLD. G1–G4 fail; G5 passes.

## Mechanism
- **The rule:** CFM's opt-in intraday margin (weekdays 8 am–4 pm ET) gives way at 4:00 pm to overnight margin 2–4× higher. Rates ramp up from 3:30, and an account that is short at 4:00 goes straight into liquidation. CDE settles on the 15:59–16:00 VWAP.
- **Who is forced:** retail longs sized on intraday margin must sell into thin books.
- **Who is paid:** a liquidity provider, or a buyer after the step, should earn the reversion.
- **The catch:** CFM may internalise liquidations (memo R1), so Step 1 is the kill test.

## Data
- **Prints:** 7,333,526 CDE perp prints in 12,244 windows. That is 19 perps × every calendar day from launch to 2026-11-05 × two ET windows (13:30–14:30 control; 15:30–17:30). Fetched with 17,130 requests on six Actions runners, with no errors.
- **Completeness:** on DEV trading days (4,236 windows, 4.69M prints), print volume matches 1-minute candle volume to within 0.005%. Only 2 windows are off by more than 1%.
- **Basis residual:** H1's definition, built from perp candles (data/cfm) and spot candles (data/minute).
- **Calendar:** the ET clock with daylight saving; weekdays minus US holidays (listed in hcommon.py).
  - 217 DEV trading days (2025-07-21 – 2026-05-29).
  - 99 weekend/holiday DEV days, used as a descriptive placebo.
- **Universe, by launch:**
  - BTC, ETH: Jul 2025.
  - SOL, XRP: Aug 2025.
  - DOGE, LTC, LINK, ADA, BCH, SUI, XLM, HBAR: Dec 2025.
  - NEAR, AAVE, ENA, PEPE, ONDO, ZEC: Mar 2026.
  - HYPE: Jun 2026 (HOLD only).
- **Excluded:** AVAX fails the $150 contract rule every day; BNB, DOT and SHIB have no candles.

## Frozen variant
**vflow_x2_all** (freeze commit 7c378c0). It had the highest DEV alpha t of the 21 trials; the runner-up was vafter z2 17:00 all at −1.56.
- **Signal:** at 16:01 ET, buy the perp of any coin whose 15:30–16:00 return is below −2× the trailing 20-day standard deviation of that return.
- **Entry:** taker, at the first print at or after 16:01:02, worse by 1.5× the half-spread.
- **Exit:** at market, at the first print at or after 16:45:02.
- **Size:** min($15K, 2% of the trailing 20-day median 15:30–16:30 perp volume); gross ≤ 3× the sleeve.

## Capacity and fill realism
- **Capacity:**
  - Mean notional $7.5K, 50% of the $15K target: about $14.5K on the majors, $0.3–4.5K on alts, where the 2%-of-volume cap binds.
  - Active on 38 of 476 days.
- **Entries and exits** are priced on actual prints:
  - The median entry is 4.3 s after 16:01:00 (p90 42 s). 12 of 127 entries came more than 60 s late, all in thin alts.
  - Each leg also pays 1.5× the median half-spread and the per-contract fee.
- **V-bid fills:**
  - Rules: prints strictly through the level, size ≤ θ × through-volume, live from t + 1 s, post-only feasibility checked, ±5% Fair Value Limit enforced.
  - 85% of fills reached the take-profit. The rest lost about 57 bp, because the bid filled while spot itself was falling (adverse selection). Gross P&L was about 0.

## Why it fails
- **Size of the footprint:** the step-up causes visible forced selling, but it moves the majors' basis only about 2–3 bp, and the move reverts slowly.
- **Cost of a round trip at 2×:**
  - About 9 bp on BTC (maker).
  - About 28 bp on ETH (maker), because $0.40 is charged per 0.1-ETH contract.
  - 12–40 bp as a taker.
- **The gross edges that do appear are too small or too rare:**
  - V-after (z ≤ −1, BTC+ETH): +10.6 bp gross, trade-level t 3.2. Smaller than costs.
  - V-flow (x = 2): +42 bp gross on 54 trades. Too rare to beat T-bills on idle capital.
- **Post hoc, not a trial:** BTC alone in V-after z ≤ −1 netted +1.8 bp at 2×. The fee on ETH's small contract is the main drag.

## Weaknesses
- The mechanism faded about 6× after Oct 2025. Its state on HOLD is unknown, because Step 1 is DEV-only by design.
- Taker fills use the next print, not the book: there is no CDE book history before 2026-10-14, and thin alts print late.
- The all-in perp fee is unverified; $0.20 per side is the assumption.
- Spot data are 1-minute closes, which adds up to a minute of timing noise to the residual.

## Deviations (none after HOLD)
1. **Print fetcher:** common.TradeFetcher.window drops prints (see below). A subclass in hcommon.py re-requests the boundary second and removes duplicates. common.py itself is unchanged.
2. **Fetch windows:** 15:30–17:30 ET instead of the SPEC's 15:30–16:45, to cover the 17:00 exits; plus the 13:30–14:30 control. Weekend and holiday aggregates are a descriptive placebo, not a trial.
3. **Step-1 choices:** USD-volume weights per day (equal weights also reported) and the window bounds were committed before step1.py first ran.
4. **Step-1 trials:** logged as the idle-sleeve DEV book, with their t-stats in `extra`.
5. **Taker sizing:** the volume measure is the 15:30–16:30 ET USD perp volume over the trailing 20 trading days.
6. **Exits and V-bid rules:** if no print comes within 30 minutes of an exit, the trade exits at the next candle's open. This and the V-bid rules above were fixed before any Step-2 number existed.
7. **PEPE prices** are quoted per 1,000 PEPE. They are converted (× 1e−3) before costs and the $150 rule.

## Shared-code problems
1. **TradeFetcher.window drops prints at page boundaries.**
   - Each next page ends at int(oldest print time), and `end` is exclusive, so earlier prints in that same second are lost.
   - In the pilot, ETH lost 4,865 of the 5,709 contracts traded in the 16:00 ET minute on 2026-03-11.
   - The losses fall in bursts, exactly where the 4 pm and wick studies look (H1 especially).
   - Fix: hcommon.Fetcher.
2. **PEPE units.**
   - common.PERP is in PEPE units, but data/cfm and the prints quote per 1,000 PEPE.
   - Passing a raw perp price makes perp_cost_bp understate PEPE fees 1,000×, and USD volume comes out 1,000× too high.
   - SHIB's tick in common.PERP (1e−9 per coin) also disagrees with the product metadata (1e−8). This is untested: there is no SHIB data.
3. **gates v_z is inflated** (ledger v_z 163), so G4 is provisional, as the coordinator noted.
