# H4 (NL): liquidity for new Coinbase listings, first 30 days

Builder h4nl, 2026-11-07, branch hunt/h4nl. Path B.
- **Freeze commit:** c912018 (frozen.json; code 0e5aa3b).
- **Verdict: FAILS G1, G2, G3 and G4; passes G5.** The sleeve is positive in every year and uncorrelated with the
  incumbent, but far too small to add Sharpe.

## Mechanism
- A new Coinbase spot book opens thin, with no market-maker inventory, while airdrop and early holders sell.
- A resting bid kσ under the 60-minute VWAP buys their sweeps. It exits post-only 60% of the way back, or at market
  after H minutes.
- These coins are never in R2-08's universe.

## Data
**Universe** (scripts/hunt/h4nl/listings.py; rules committed before any price was fetched). Of 496 USD products in
Exchange /products (any status), these were excluded:
- 127 first traded before 2022;
- 15 stablecoins or asset-backed tokens;
- 10 wrapped or staked tokens;
- 10 ticker migrations (T, RENDER, POL, SYRUP, SKY, CFG, G, MANTLE, ZETACHAIN, AXL).

**Listings:** 334 remain (2022: 97, 2023: 24, 2024: 43, 2025: 101, 2026: 69).
- **43 are delisted now**, 31 of them from the 2022 cohort.
- 333 have minute data.

**t0** = the first 1-minute candle with volume.
- It always falls on the first daily-candle day.
- Against Advanced Trade's `new_at`: 215 of 219 t0 values fall 0–72 h after it (median 20 h).

**Candles and sample:**
- **Candles:** 1-minute, days 0–31, 7.4M rows, from 8 Actions shards.
- **Candidate fills:** 2,711 on DEV.
- **Trade-print sample:** a fixed-seed 25% of DEV fill minutes, 553 windows. Delisted products were fetched by
  Exchange trade-id paging.

**Scoring:** gates.ERA_DAYS. 2024 listings only train the AI.

## Simulation (rules fixed in sim.py before any trial)
- **Bid:** F = 60-minute VWAP. σ = the std of 1-minute log returns over the trailing min(1,440, minutes since t0),
  × √60. Both are known at t−1. The bid is F·e^(−kσ), floored to the tick. Warm-up is 2 h.
- **Entry:** the minute opens above the bid and trades through it by at least 1 tick.
  - Size: min($3K, θ × candle through-volume), θ = 0.5.
- **Exits:**
  - The take-profit is capped at θ × the volume traded above it.
  - The rest is sold at the next traded open after t+H.
- **Costs:** maker 6 bp, taker 12.5 bp, plus a market-exit half-spread of 5 bp. Gates use 2×.
- **Limits:**
  - At most 6 positions, one per coin.
  - Resting bids reserve cash; the newest listings come first.
  - Peak cash: $3.6K of $30K for the frozen variant.

## Fill realism (DEV trade sample)
**Coverage:**
- In 535 of 682 levels, the prints' USD volume matched the candle's (within 10%).
- The rest had incomplete prints, a data gap.

**Confirmation:**
- With complete prints, **96.8%** of candle fills are confirmed by a print at least one tick through the level.
- With incomplete prints, 48%.
- Overall, 86%.

**Through-volume:**
- With complete, confirmed prints, the print through-volume is a median **0.32×** the candle estimate (IQR
  0.03–0.96; 0.26× in aggregate).
- Spreading candle volume evenly over the range badly overstates the volume available at the lows.

**Frozen trades in the sample** (23 with complete prints):
- Capping each fill at θ × the print through-volume cuts the filled dollars from $48K to $25K.
- It cuts P&L from $1,403 to $231.
- **So the candle-based P&L likely overstates live P&L by 2–6×.**

**Spreads** (adjacent opposite-side prints within 2 s):
- Half-spread: median 6.0 bp, mean 12.2 bp, p90 28 bp.
- At the frozen variant's fills: median 9.9 bp, mean 22 bp.
- So the SPEC's 10 bp at 2× is only median-level.

**Depth:**
- A median $3.9K of seller sweep per fill minute, which moved price a median 158 bp (about $32 per bp).
- The median tick is 0.67 bp.

## Weaknesses
- **Too small:** about 5–6% a year on $30K. Taking 30% of capital from a Sharpe-2.4 incumbent costs more than the
  sleeve adds.
- **Fills:** candle fills overstate size badly (print through-volume is about 0.3×).
- **Selection noise:**
  - The AI filter's improvement is within noise (t 1.04 → 1.21).
  - The selection maximum came from a mostly negative grid.
- **Listing phases not modelled:** limit-only phases, 14–24 h after the auction, reject post-only orders.
- **Half-spread tail:** fatter than modelled (mean 22 bp at fills).

## Deviations
- **Exclusion lists:** four migrations (G, MANTLE, ZETACHAIN, AXL) and a carve-out for CHIP (a governance token named
  "USD.AI") were added to the fixed lists. They came from reading names and first trading days only, before any
  price.
- **Shared code:** after my trade runs, the coordinator fixed common.TradeFetcher (ISO8601 parsing, a delisted
  fallback). My runs used their own Exchange fallback for delisted products, so nothing changed.
- **G4:** provisional, per the coordinator.
- **After HOLD:** no change.

## Verdict
**Fails.** There is a real but small, front-loaded effect in a listing's first week, positive in every year even at
2× costs. It cannot reach t ≥ 3 or a +0.5 Sharpe gain, and print-level fills shrink it further. Not worth a sleeve.
