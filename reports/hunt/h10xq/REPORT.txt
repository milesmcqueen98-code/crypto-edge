# H10 (XQ): secondary-book wicks — resting bids in Coinbase's BTC-, ETH- and USDT-quoted books

Verdict: FAILS every gate. All 13 DEV trials lose heavily at 2× and at 1× costs. The bids are filled by
stale-quote pick-offs, not by forced sellers. Frozen variant: k6_b_BTCETH. Path B at 2× costs: G1 t = −10.87,
G2 HOLD t = −9.31 with a HOLD Sharpe gain of −7.67, G3 gain −11.49, G4 DSR ≈ 0 (provisional), G5 every year
negative.

## Mechanism (SPEC) and what the data say
- **The SPEC's idea.** In cascades, market makers pull secondary-book quotes first. A resting bid at
  F·exp(−kσ_c) buys from swept sellers. F is the USD-implied cross at t−1 times the trailing 24 h median residual.
  σ_c is the trailing 3-day std of the 1-minute residual.
- **What happens.** In the books that get the bid slots (ETH-BTC, SOL-BTC, BTC/ETH/SOL-USDT …), σ_c is 3–10 bp,
  mostly noise from asynchronous closes. A 6σ bid therefore sits only ~28 bp below a fair value that is up to one
  minute stale. The bid fills when the USD books have already moved and the cross book follows.
- **What the fill locks in.** For the frozen variant, the residual locked in against the USD books at the hedge
  averages +1.7 bp on DEV (median +1.0 bp; +3.0 bp on HOLD). The round trip costs 70 bp at 2× (35 bp at 1×).
  - Only 1.9% of fills lock more than the 2× cost.
  - Fills ≥ 100 bp below F (547) lock 35 bp on average and still lose.
  - The large true dislocations exist: WLUNA-USDT −82% (2022-05-12), HBAR-USDT −55%, XRP-USDT −39% and DOGE-USDT
    −33% (2025-10-10). They are a handful of minutes, swamped by thousands of adverse fills.

## Other SPEC checks
- **Overlap with the incumbent.**
  - 33% of the sleeve's trade days are incumbent fill days, and 23% of its trades fall on the same day and coin.
  - It loses on both: −$327K on incumbent fill days, −$260K on other days.
- **Correlation with the incumbent.** −0.19 (beta −1.0). The sleeve loses most on cascade days, which are the
  incumbent's best days. Its sum over the incumbent's 20 worst days is −90% of its capital.
- **Concentration.**
  - The 3 worst days carry 5.5% of losses: 2022-01-21/22 and 2025-02-03.
  - Positive days: 0.2%. Best day +1.8% (2023-11-12). The top-3-days share of P&L is not defined, since P&L is
    negative.
- **Mark-to-market.** No overnight directional exposure: exit (b) flattens within a minute, and the inventory is
  hedged with the perp short.

## Weaknesses and limitations
- **Print verification covers a sample only.** Every gate is on candles; see the deviations.
- **Historical ticks.** The current quote_increment is used for all history. Coarser historical ticks would make
  the one-tick trade-through slightly harder, so this is mildly optimistic.
- **Pre-July 2025 hedge.** The perp hedge before Jul 2025 is priced with the perp cost model on spot prices
  (flagged, per the SPEC). Funding at 1 bp/8 h on either side costs 11%/yr at 1× (22% at 2×) on the inventory.
  This is negligible next to the trade losses.
- **Fixed-capital returns.** Returns ignore ruin, which flatters the sleeve's risk.

## Deviations
1. **Print verification sampled.** Prints were fetched for a seeded sample (about 1% of fills), not every
   candidate window. The 13 trials produce 1.41M fills, which was infeasible under a shared 20-job
   Actions queue. All scoring (trials, freeze, gates) uses candles for every fill with costs exactly as in
   common.py, one consistent basis. The prints quantify the bias (H10_USE_PRINTS=1 applies them, for information).
2. **Shared-code fixes pulled before any print run** (coordinator):
   - cfbe931: ISO8601 timestamps; Exchange trade-id fallback for delisted products.
   - 06a9416: loss-free paging of trades.

   No print verification ran on the old fetcher. Coinbase's Advanced trades endpoint returns 404 for delisted
   pairs (probe).
3. **G4 is provisional.** It is recomputed centrally (prereg deviation 2026-11-07 06:00).
4. **Engineering choices.** These are not in the SPEC:
   - Daily bid slots go by trailing ADV rank.
   - Fills under $100 are ignored.
   - c̄ needs ≥ 30 joint minutes and σ_c ≥ 120.
   - Leg closes are forward-filled ≤ 30 minutes; statistics are carried ≤ 6 h.
   - Take-profit volume is counted over the holding window (as in the incumbent's simulator).
   - The cascade σ is the trailing 3-day std of 15-minute moves.
   - The H = 30 variant was not run (it doesn't apply to exit (b)).

## Verdict
FAIL on G1–G5 (G6 moot). The pre-registered bid level is priced off a USD fair value that is up to a minute stale,
inside the noise band of liquid secondary books. It is adversely selected: the average locked-in cross
dislocation is 1–3 bp against 35–70 bp of costs. The real multi-percent breaks (Oct 10 2025, May 2022) are too
rare to matter. A redesign (sub-second fair value, much larger k, cascade-only quoting with the hedge on prints)
would be a new idea with its own pre-registration, not a fix.
