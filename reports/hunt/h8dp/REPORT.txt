# H8 (h8dp): depeg bids under par for stablecoins and cbETH on Coinbase. Verdict: FAILS (G1-G4)

## Mechanism
In a scare, holders sell stablecoins (or wrapped/staked assets) below par. Funds de-risk, and holders who cannot
wait for redemption sell. Resting bids at par x (1 - d) buy that flow and sell at par - 5 bp after recovery. The
risk is a true default. Per SPEC, cbETH's "par" = trailing 30-day median cbETH/ETH ratio x ETH's previous-minute close.

## Frozen variant
- **Variant:** all, deep, tp.
  - Bids 50/100/200 bp under par on USDT, DAI, PYUSD, GUSD, USDS and cbETH.
  - Cash is split equally across the pairs listed that day and across the rungs.
  - Exit: post-only sale at par - 5 bp only.
- **Selection rule:** the highest DEV alpha t with at least 20 fills.
- **Freeze commit:** 0b70ec7 (code 0b8bb8f). HOLD was read once afterwards.

## Capacity actually filled
- Time-weighted deployment is 3.1% of sleeve capital; the ladder is idle about 97% of the time.
- Stablecoin rungs fill completely in USDT and DAI events ($5K-$25K bought per episode). cbETH rungs fill partly.
- At theta = 0.25, total bought falls from $8.8M to $7.3M.

## Fill-realism checks
- **Entries:** fills only on prints at least one tick through the bid; size ≤ theta x through-volume.
  - Rungs share sellers by the wick1m rule: each rung is capped by its own through-volume, and all rungs together by
    the shallowest rung's.
- **Exits:** only prints at least one tick above the ask, with no same-minute round trips.
- **Latency:** levels use information at least 1 minute old (cbETH par: prior-day ratio x ETH's close the minute
  before).
- **Trade side:** the Exchange /trades `side` is the maker side, confirmed by tick test.
  - The fetcher first assumed the taker side. This was fixed before any use.
- **Delistings:** DAI, GUSD and PYUSD are liquidated at their last close with market-order costs.
- **Mechanics check:** a UST run confirmed the accounting identities.

## Power calculation
- **Frozen variant:**
  - Over 1,404 scored days, the daily standard deviation is 0.27%.
  - t = 3 needs 7.8% a year of excess return, i.e. an 11.5% sleeve return. The sleeve made 5.9%.
- **Stablecoin-only ladder (usdt, deep):**
  - It needs 5.2% a year: about 36 active days a year at the observed payoff, against 6.4 observed.
- **Structural ceiling:**
  - About 3 independent stress events give t ≤ √(events) ≈ 1.7, even with identical payoffs and no T-bill hurdle.
- **Conclusion:** on Coinbase's history, a stablecoin depeg sleeve cannot reach G1 by construction.

## Worst case: no recovery (UST stress; described, not traded)
- **The SPEC's premise is wrong:** UST did trade on Coinbase (UST-USD, 2021-08-12 to 2022-05-27).
- **Stress, deep ladder with $10K a rung:** every rung fills on May 7-9, 2022, in at least $0.4M an hour of selling.
  - Take-profit-only exit: held to delisting at 0.033, -96.7% of the sleeve.
  - 7-day exit: -85.9%.
- **Simulated with the candle rule:** -31% / -26%. Gapped minutes don't fill, which understates the loss.
- **Survivorship:** the SPEC universe is survivors chosen in 2026. One default erases about 85 years of the
  stablecoin premium earned here.

## Weaknesses
1. The frozen sleeve's edge is cbETH relative value, not stablecoin depegs. It decayed and lost on HOLD.
2. Survivorship: UST is excluded, and the strategy is short the default tail.
3. Fees on stable pairs are unverified (third-party). USDT pays VIP 1, which makes shallow rungs uneconomic.
4. The ladder sits idle against T-bills, and cash held for resting bids earns nothing.
5. P&L is booked at exit. The marked-to-market maximum drawdown is -11.2% of the sleeve (the 2025-26 bleed plus open
   cbETH).
6. The cbETH par needs a re-quote every minute (operational load). Its stale-par fills are adversely selected.

## Deviations
All were made before the freeze unless noted.
- cbBTC is omitted (no Coinbase spot book).
- Stable-pair fees follow R3 per the coordinator; USDT and cbETH use VIP 1.
- GUSD's market-order half-spread is half its 10 bp tick (5 bp).
- Engineering choices:
  - the wick1m seller-sharing rule;
  - candle exits without the open condition (only outside print windows);
  - Exchange full trade history for PYUSD, GUSD and USDS.
- A "fill" for the 20-fill rule is a rung round trip.
- The UST stress was computed analytically (above).
- Post-freeze: episodes_all.csv and the reporting checks only. No change to code or variant.

## Verdict
**FAILS (G1, G2, G3, G4).**
- Depeg bidding on Coinbase is crisis-liquidity insurance with a short default tail, not alpha. It sees about three
  real events in 5.5 years, earns below T-bills, and survivorship hides the UST loss.
- The only positive DEV signal (cbETH discount bids) was a 2022-23 artifact that reversed out of sample.
- Do not carry it to wave 3.
